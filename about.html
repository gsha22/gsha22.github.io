bio

Hello again! I'm a software engineer at Bloomberg and a Carnegie Mellon University graduate.

I studied IS + CS because I love both the theoretical and practical side of technology. I think that being at CMU with this combination of majors gave me a unique perspective because I learned to understand the beauty of theoretical computer science, algorithms, and math, dove deep in complicated systems projects to learn how to become a better programmer, and also discovered how to sometimes ditch complexity and go with the most practical approach. What I eventually realized throughout tackling these different challenges is how everything I did helped me develop my problem solving skills, and how fun it can be.

Something else about me is I love to teach. To me there are very few feelings better than helping someone who was really struggling finally be able to bridge the gap in their understanding and feel like they've truly learned something new. I believe that a lot of who I am today comes from the mentors and educators that invested in me, and I try my best to do the same for those around me. At school I was a teaching assistant for the intro the CS class 15-112, which often was CMU students first taste of computer science, and also for 15-440: Distributed Systems, where many students learned how to become better engineers.

I love problem solving and I love constantly collaborating with other people, so becoming a software engineer felt like the natural choice. I'm excited to be at Bloomberg to learn as much as possible from everyone around me.

I'm always open to chat, so if we have anything in common and/or there is anything you may want to talk about, feel free to reach out!
